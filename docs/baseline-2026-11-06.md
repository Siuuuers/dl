## Results

Settings were fixed at `imgsz=1280`, `rect=True`, batch 1, `nms=True`, NMS IoU 0.70, `max_det=300`, and FP32 via `quantize=32`. Evaluation matched boxes at IoU at least 0.50. Endpoint confidence remained 0.50 throughout.

| Box confidence | Matched objects (TP) | Missed objects (FN) | Extra predictions (FP) | Duplicate / other FP | Returned / 1,227 visible endpoints | Coverage | Conditional median error | Conditional p95 error |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.15 | 631 | 33 | 90 | 23 / 67 | 1,189 | 96.90% | 1.62 px | 5.24 px |
| 0.25 | 625 | 39 | 63 | 17 / 46 | 1,184 | 96.50% | 1.61 px | 5.16 px |
| 0.40 | 622 | 42 | 44 | 8 / 36 | 1,181 | 96.25% | 1.61 px | 5.17 px |

The three empty scenes produced zero detections at all three thresholds. Three examples are a useful check, not a broad estimate of false-alarm performance in arbitrary empty scenes.

At 0.25, the model returned 688 predictions: 625 matched and 63 extra under the declared matcher. Of 664 reference objects, 39 remained unmatched. All **43 missing scored endpoints** belonged to unmatched reference objects. **Zero** visible endpoints were lost to low endpoint confidence, nonfinite coordinates or the frame-boundary rule after their object had matched.

Changing 0.25 to 0.40 removed 19 FPs, including 9 duplicates, at the cost of 3 additional FNs and 3 fewer returned visible endpoints. Changing 0.25 to 0.15 recovered 6 matched objects and 5 visible endpoints but added 27 FPs. Conditional pixel errors changed little. The small apparent median improvement at higher confidence is not proof of better localization: the included set of matched objects can change.

Keep **0.25 as the recorded diagnostic reference**. Treat 0.40 as a candidate when extra detections are costly, not as an established deployment optimum. The relative costs of an extra object and a missed object have not been specified. Lowering the endpoint-confidence cutoff is not supported by the default run's missing-endpoint accounting.

These are additional **diagnostic comparisons**, not accepted matches and not extra observations in the headline error statistics. All seven remain FN/FP under the fixed matching rule. They show box-localization/extent discrepancies; they do not prove that the reference labels are wrong. Lowering box confidence will not fix these already confident proposals.

For example, `new_000090` row 4 has a reference box about 15.4 pixels high and a nearby predicted box about 38.6 pixels high. Its corresponding corners differ by only about 1.5 pixels. The measurement separates an extent disagreement from absence of a useful corner proposal.

There are also genuine corner-position discrepancies within accepted object matches. At confidence 0.25, `new_000008` has a conditional median of 4.45 px and p95 of 8.34 px. Its reference row 10, right endpoint, has a **14.85 px** error against prediction row 28 despite box IoU 0.821 and endpoint confidence 0.934. This example demonstrates why endpoint confidence must not be interpreted as pixel accuracy. The separate replay overlay includes this frame for inspection; it does not change the primary three-image box-review task.

## Limits on interpretation

- These 20 images already helped select epoch 173. They are development validation, not an independent final test. Final generalization requires the excluded final-test data or genuinely new independent scenes.
- The pixel median and p95 are conditional on matched objects and returned, reference-visible endpoints. At 0.25 that is 1,184 endpoints out of 1,227; missing endpoints are not assigned a zero error. These numbers do not mean that every physical corner is within 5.16 pixels.
- This evaluator implements the declared fixed-threshold object matcher and pixel diagnostics. It does not reproduce or recompute the upstream precision/recall/mAP evaluation, so its counts are not interchangeable with the model-card metrics.
- Saved outputs have already been scaled and clipped by Ultralytics. They cannot reveal pre-clipping network coordinates.
- No pixel/millimetre acceptance tolerance, camera calibration or cost weighting between misses and extras has been supplied. No physical positioning or deployment acceptance claim is made.
- The run measures the unchanged supplied checkpoint. There was no training, architecture change, reference relabeling or independent browser acceptance in this milestone.
