# Final Year Project I report requirements

The report is for undergraduate Final Year Project I in the Department of Electronic Engineering, followed by Project II next semester. It explains the vision task for an unfamiliar reader. Acknowledgements are omitted at the user's request. No institution-specific cover template, word limit or page limit has been supplied.

| Requirement | Current report |

## Presentation and evidence

- All 25 sections and subsections have decimal numbers and contents entries.
- Six major equations are centered, editable Word equations with right-aligned numbers.
- One figure and four tables have numbered explanatory captions.
- Physical units are metric; image-coordinate errors are stated in pixels.
- The untouched supplied checkpoint is the common baseline in the report. The original and proposed methods are freshly evaluated in the same runtime and at the same operating thresholds.
- The manuscript explains the project's procedures and conclusions directly. It contains no GitHub links, self-citations to project notes, or unexplained revision names.
- External publications support theories and methodological ideas. Technical provenance remains in the separate source map and evidence folders.
- Completed inference, completed local-model training, synthetic numerical checks, and the untrained residual-correction hypothesis are distinguished.
- The earlier annotation result remains visible because its direction differs from the reviewed-reference result.

The Word report has 14 pages in a simple academic layout: Letter portrait, 25 mm margins, Times New Roman 12-point body text, 1.15 line spacing, black headings and page numbers. The body flows between major sections; the front matter, Introduction, References and Appendices begin on fresh pages. The validation record documents the final contents map and visual inspection of every page. An institutional template can be applied later.
