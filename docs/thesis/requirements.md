## Presentation and evidence

- All 40 sections and subsections receive decimal numbers and contents entries.
- Nine major equations are numbered and stored as editable Word equations.
- Four figures and 11 tables have descriptive numbered captions.
- Physical units are metric; pixel distances describe image coordinates.
- External publications support theories and proposed methods. The thesis explains the cassette experiments, measurement rules, results, and reasoning directly, with internal section and table references where useful.
- The manuscript and Word report contain no GitHub links, project-record citations, or unexplained internal revision names. The source map and evidence files provide technical provenance separately.
- Terms and abbreviations are introduced before use. Descriptive experiment and annotation-reference names replace internal codes in report prose, tables, and figure labels.
- Completed model experiments, analyses of saved predictions, and proposed training are distinguished. Reflection and coordinate averaging are complete; the new local-refinement GPU training results remain pending.

The verified Word report has 29 pages in a simple academic layout: Letter portrait, 25 mm margins, Times New Roman, black headings, and numbered pages.
